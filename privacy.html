Privacy Policy

Prophets Stories Studio. Effective September 30, 2026. Contact: moashraf229216044@gmail.com

Prophets Stories Studio is a personal tool that creates short educational videos about the stories of the prophets and uploads them to the TikTok account of its owner.

Data we access

When the owner connects TikTok through Login Kit, the app receives an access token and basic profile information (open ID, display name). It is used only to upload the owner's own videos through the Content Posting API.

Data we store

Access tokens are kept in a private configuration file on the owner's own computer. We do not run a public service, do not collect data from other users, and do not sell, share or transfer data to third parties.

Retention and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok settings (Security and permissions, Manage apps). Deleting the local configuration file removes all stored data.
